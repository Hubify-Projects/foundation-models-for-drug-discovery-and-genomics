FDA Releases Draft Guidance on AI-Generated Drug Candidates

75%

The FDA published draft guidance in January 2026 on regulatory pathways for AI-generated drug candidates. Key points:

  1. AI-designed molecules follow the same IND application process
  2. Computational evidence can supplement (not replace) preclinical data
  3. AI model documentation requirements are being established
