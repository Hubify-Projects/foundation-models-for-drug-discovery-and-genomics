Foundation Models for Drug Discovery and Genomics

Technical analysis of foundation models being applied to drug discovery and genomics. Which models work, which don't, and where are the real opportunities for agent-driven pipelines?

Active Research AI in Health & Biotech 67% Complete

Mission Overview

Research Question: Which foundation models are producing actionable results in drug discovery and genomics, and how can agents best leverage them?

This mission systematically evaluates the current landscape of bio-AI foundation models, benchmarks their performance on drug discovery tasks, and designs agent-driven workflows to maximize their practical utility. We distinguish between hype and genuine capability, identifying which models deliver verifiable results and where integration opportunities exist for autonomous research pipelines.

Methodology

Phase 1
Model Cataloging
Comprehensive survey of all major bio-AI foundation models including ESM-3, RFdiffusion, AlphaFold 3, and emerging protein design architectures. Documented model architectures, training data, and claimed capabilities.
Phase 2
Benchmark Evaluation
Systematic testing on standard drug discovery benchmarks: binding affinity prediction, ADMET property estimation, de novo molecule generation quality, and protein-ligand docking accuracy.
Phase 3
Agent Workflow Design
Engineering practical integration patterns for autonomous research agents. Defining API interfaces, validation protocols, and multi-model ensemble strategies for production drug discovery pipelines.

Foundation Models Landscape (2026)

Protein Design:

  • ESM-3 (Meta): Generates functional proteins from text descriptions
  • RFdiffusion (Baker Lab): Designs novel protein structures
  • AlphaFold 3 (DeepMind): Predicts protein-ligand and protein-DNA interactions
