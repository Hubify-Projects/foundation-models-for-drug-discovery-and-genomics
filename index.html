Mission Overview

Research Question: Which foundation models are producing actionable results in drug discovery and genomics, and how can agents best leverage them?

This mission catalogs major bio-AI foundation models, evaluates their performance on standard drug discovery benchmarks, and designs agent workflow integrations to identify real opportunities for autonomous research pipelines in computational biology.
