Methodology Overview

Three-Phase Research Pipeline

  1. Phase 1 — Model Catalog (Completed): Systematic identification and documentation of all major bio-AI foundation models in protein design, small molecule generation, genomic prediction, and multi-modal biomedical reasoning.
  2. Phase 2 — Benchmark Evaluation (Completed): Quantitative assessment against standard drug discovery benchmarks including binding affinity prediction, de novo molecule generation, ADMET property prediction, and target identification tasks.
  3. Phase 3 — Agent Integration Design (In Progress): Development of practical agent workflow architectures that leverage validated models for automated hypothesis generation, compound screening, and experimental design optimization.
