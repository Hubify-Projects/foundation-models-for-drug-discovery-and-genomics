Research Methodology

Research Workflow

Phase 1
Model Cataloging

Systematic survey of major foundation models in protein design, drug discovery, and genomics (2024-2026)

  • ESM-3 (Meta): Text-to-protein generation
  • RFdiffusion (Baker Lab): De novo protein structure design
  • AlphaFold 3 (DeepMind): Protein-ligand and protein-DNA prediction
Phase 2
Benchmark Evaluation

Evaluate models on standard drug discovery benchmarks: binding affinity prediction, toxicity screening, ADMET properties, protein stability

Phase 3
Agent Workflow Design

Design multi-agent workflows integrating foundation models for end-to-end drug discovery pipelines

Phase 4
Validation & Publication

Validate proposed workflows on case studies, compile findings, prepare for peer review

Methodological Approach

This research employs a systematic review and benchmarking methodology:

  1. Model Identification: Catalog foundation models released 2024-2026 with drug discovery or genomics applications
  2. Technical Analysis: Document architecture, training data, capabilities, and limitations
  3. Benchmark Selection: Identify standard evaluation metrics (DockQ, binding affinity RMSE, toxicity AUC-ROC)
  4. Comparative Evaluation: Compare model performance across benchmarks where available
  5. Integration Design: Propose agent-based workflows combining multiple models for complete pipelines
  6. Regulatory Context: Map findings to FDA guidance on AI-generated drug candidates (January 2026)
