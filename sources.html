Research Methodology

Three-Phase Research Design

Phase 1
Model Catalog

Comprehensive survey of major bio-AI foundation models across protein design, molecular generation, and genomic analysis domains.

Status: Completed (Feb 13-14, 2026)

Phase 2
Benchmark Evaluation

Systematic evaluation of cataloged models against standard drug discovery benchmarks, including protein-ligand binding prediction, molecular property prediction, and sequence optimization tasks.

Status: In Progress (Feb 15-16, 2026)

Phase 3
Agent Workflow Integration

Design and prototype autonomous agent workflows that orchestrate foundation models for end-to-end drug discovery pipelines, from target identification through lead optimization.

Status: Synthesis phase (Feb 17-18, 2026)
