Research Methodology

Phase-Based Research Approach

This research mission follows a structured, multi-phase methodology designed to systematically catalog, evaluate, and integrate foundation models for drug discovery and genomics applications.

Phase 1
Model Cataloging

Comprehensive survey of major bio-AI foundation models including protein design tools (ESM-3, RFdiffusion, AlphaFold 3), small molecule generation models, and genomic interpretation frameworks.

Completed
Phase 2
Benchmark Evaluation

Assessment of cataloged models against standard drug discovery benchmarks, including binding affinity prediction, ADMET property estimation, and de novo molecule generation tasks.

Completed
Phase 3
Agent Workflow Integration

Design and validation of multi-agent workflows that combine foundation models for end-to-end drug discovery pipelines, from target identification through lead optimization.

In Progress

Primary Sources

Foundation Models

  1. ESM-3 (Meta) — Evolutionary Scale Modeling v3: Protein language model for functional protein generation from text descriptions
    Meta AI Research, 2024-2025
  2. RFdiffusion (Baker Lab) — Diffusion-based generative model for novel protein structure design
    Watson et al., Institute for Protein Design, University of Washington
  3. AlphaFold 3 (DeepMind) — Advanced protein structure prediction including protein-ligand and protein-DNA interactions
    Google DeepMind, 2024

Regulatory Sources

  1. FDA Draft Guidance on AI-Generated Drug Candidates — Regulatory pathways for AI-designed molecules, computational evidence requirements, and AI model documentation standards
    U.S. Food and Drug Administration, January 2026

    Key findings: AI-designed molecules follow standard IND application process; computational evidence supplements but does not replace preclinical data; specific AI model documentation requirements established for regulatory review.

Data Sources

Datasets Referenced

While specific benchmark datasets are evaluated in Phase 2 analysis, this research primarily focuses on model architecture analysis and integration design rather than novel dataset creation. Standard drug discovery datasets include:

  • Protein Data Bank (PDB) — structural biology reference
  • ChEMBL — bioactivity database for drug-like molecules
  • PubChem — chemical compound information
  • UniProt — protein sequence and functional information

These datasets are publicly available and commonly used for foundation model training and evaluation. No proprietary datasets were accessed for this research.
