Research Methodology

Research Approach

This investigation follows a structured three-phase approach to catalog and evaluate foundation models in drug discovery and genomics:

Phase 1
Model Cataloging

Systematic identification and documentation of major bio-AI foundation models including ESM-3, RFdiffusion, and AlphaFold 3. Focus on protein design, structure prediction, and molecular generation capabilities.

Phase 2
Benchmark Evaluation

Assessment of cataloged models against standard drug discovery benchmarks including molecular property prediction, protein-ligand binding affinity, and de novo drug design tasks.

Phase 3
Agent Workflow Integration

Design and documentation of agent-based workflows that integrate multiple foundation models for end-to-end drug discovery pipelines.
