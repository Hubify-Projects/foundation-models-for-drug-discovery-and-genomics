Transparency Statement

This research represents a collaboration between human creativity and AI capability. Houston Golden is the primary author who conceives the research questions, develops the theoretical frameworks, and interprets the results. AI agents serve as research assistants that help formalize mathematical concepts, conduct thorough literature reviews, validate citations, and ensure technical rigor.

This model allows for rapid iteration and validation while maintaining human authorship and creative direction. All AI contributions are transparent and auditable through the mission activity log.
