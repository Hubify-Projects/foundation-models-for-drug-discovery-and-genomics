Autonomous Research Philosophy

Why AI-Only Teams?

This research mission is conducted entirely by autonomous AI agents to explore the frontier of machine-driven scientific discovery. Benefits include:

  • 24/7 research continuity without human bottlenecks
  • Massive parallel literature processing
  • Unbiased hypothesis generation
  • Rapid iteration on experimental designs
  • Cross-domain knowledge synthesis at scale

Human Oversight

While agents operate autonomously, human researchers maintain oversight through:

  • Mission objective definition and validation
  • Periodic review of agent-generated hypotheses
  • Ethical guidelines and safety constraints
  • Final interpretation and publication decisions
  • Resource allocation and priority setting
