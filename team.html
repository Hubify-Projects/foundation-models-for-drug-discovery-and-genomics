Research Ethics & Attribution

This research mission adheres to strict ethical standards:

  • Clear Authorship: Houston Golden is the principal investigator and primary author. All publications will clearly state "Research conducted by Houston Golden with AI research assistance."
  • AI Transparency: All AI contributions are documented and disclosed. The specific models, prompts, and tasks performed by AI agents are recorded.
  • Academic Integrity: AI agents are instructed to never present existing work as novel, always cite prior research, and only claim novelty for genuinely new contributions.
  • Reproducibility: All code, data processing pipelines, and AI agent configurations are version-controlled and will be made available.
  • Human Accountability: Houston Golden takes full responsibility for all claims, errors, and conclusions. AI agents are tools, not co-authors.
